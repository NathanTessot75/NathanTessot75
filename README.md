I'm Nathan, a data science student at PSL University in Paris, in the CPES programme (a three-year interdisciplinary bachelor's run with Lycée Louis-le-Grand). I split my time between data science and cultural sciences, so most of my projects end up somewhere between statistics and the humanities: language, culture, and the way people rate and talk about things.

**Research projects**

- [emotions-language](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/emotions-language): do emotion words that are used in similar contexts also feel similar? Word2Vec embeddings compared with valence, arousal and dominance norms.
- [google-maps-reviews](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/google-maps-reviews): what actually gets a Paris restaurant five stars. I used an LLM to annotate 5,000 Google Maps reviews and ran regressions on the result.
- [tiktok-analysis](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/tiktok-analysis): predicting the likes of a TikTok video from its metadata, with linear models and a small neural network.
- [markov-chains](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/markov-chains): an interactive Markov chain explorer that grew out of a group paper on the topic. You can [try it here](https://nathantessot75.github.io/markov-chains/).

**Things I've built**

- [Wavemap](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/wavemap): click a country on a globe and listen to its radio. Live at [wavemap.net](https://wavemap.net).
- [Episteme](https://github.com/NathanTessot75/episteme): upload a scientific paper and get a clear explanation of it.

I mostly work in Python (pandas, scikit-learn, statsmodels, gensim), with JavaScript and Node for the web projects, and I write my reports in LaTeX.
